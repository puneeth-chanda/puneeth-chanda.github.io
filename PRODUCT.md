# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, engineering leads, collaborators, and tech community visitors learning about Puneeth Chanda's work, skills, and approach to building software.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that communicates Puneeth Chanda's identity as an engineer, designer, and builder through restrained, craft-focused design. Success looks like a visitor leaving with a clear sense of who he is, what he builds, and how he thinks about software.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, technical, craft — restrained but intentional, developer-authentic, detail-obsessed without being fussy.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS portfolio tropes — no gradient heroes, glassmorphism, bold numbered stats, template-looking designs, "we build X" mission statements, or overproduced agency gloss.

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint as signal** — every element earns its place. Nothing decorative for its own sake.
2. **Substance over flourish** — the work speaks; the design gets out of its way.
3. **Technical authenticity** — the site feels built by the engineer it represents (clean code, fast, no bloat).
4. **Quiet confidence** — no shouting, no hype. The craft is the point.
5. **Coherent across contexts** — works beautifully in light and dark, on any screen, with motion preferences respected.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

General best practices: semantic HTML, sufficient contrast, reduced motion support, keyboard navigation. No strict WCAG level target beyond baseline AA.
